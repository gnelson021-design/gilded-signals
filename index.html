Frequently Asked Questions

What does the scanner actually show?
The scanner returns price, daily change, volume, relative volume, RSI, EMA status, momentum score, market cap, and a signal strength rating for any searched ticker. Current scanner results use demo/sample data until live API integration is active.
Is this financial advice?
No. Gilded Signals is a market research and intelligence tool. All signals are informational only. Always do your own research and consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
